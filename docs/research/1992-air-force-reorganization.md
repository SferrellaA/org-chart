# 1992 Air Force Reorganization Research Ledger

## Scope

This ledger supports a renderer-review fixture, not an exact single-day order of battle. It follows Regular Air Force wings assigned to Strategic Air Command (SAC) or Tactical Air Command (TAC) during the final reorganization period into the initial Air Combat Command (ACC) and Air Mobility Command (AMC) structure.

Included echelons are MAJCOM, numbered air force, air division, and wing. Air National Guard, Air Force Reserve, provisional organizations, groups, squadrons, and leadership are excluded. Wing is the lowest displayed echelon.

The baseline combines the last documented pre-reorganization relationships needed to explain the transition. Effective dates varied through 1991 and May 1992, so it is labeled "Representative pre-reorganization structure" rather than given a false single effective date. The after state represents the 1 June 1992 command transition while retaining inherited NAF groupings for a readable comparison.

## Source Key

- **ACC lineage:** Air Force Historical Research Agency, [Air Combat Command (USAF)](https://www.dafhistory.af.mil/About-Us/Fact-Sheets/Display/Article/432046/air-combat-command-usaf/). Confirms TAC inactivation, ACC activation, inherited missions, and initial NAF components.
- **ACC history:** Air Combat Command, [ACC History](https://www.acc.af.mil/About-Us/ACC-History/). Describes the 1 June 1992 TAC/SAC consolidation and initial ACC mission set.
- **AMC lineage:** Air Force Historical Research Agency, [Air Mobility Command](https://www.dafhistory.af.mil/About-Us/Fact-Sheets/Display/Article/968648/air-mobility-command/). Confirms AMC activation, SAC tanker inheritance, and Fifteenth Air Force assignment.
- **Twelfth lineage:** Air Force Historical Research Agency, [Twelfth Air Force (Air Forces Southern)](https://www.dafhistory.af.mil/About-Us/Fact-Sheets/Display/Article/432165/twelfth-air-force-air-forces-southern-acc/). Provides dated Air Division and wing component assignments.
- **552 lineage:** Air Force Historical Research Agency, [552 Air Control Wing (ACC)](https://www.dafhistory.af.mil/About-Us/Fact-Sheets/Display/Article/432944/552-air-control-wing-acc/). Confirms 28th Air Division, TAC, ACC, and later Second Air Force assignments.
- **552 history:** 552nd Air Control Wing, [Heritage Pamphlet](https://www.552acw.acc.af.mil/Portals/109/Docs/AFD-101209-036.pdf). Describes the 28th Air Division inactivation on 29 May 1992 and wing transfer to ACC on 1 June.
- **Selected Works:** General Merrill A. McPeak, [Selected Works 1990-1994](https://apps.dtic.mil/sti/citations/ADA421979). Includes contemporary Air Division, numbered air force, and Objective Wing restructuring diagrams.
- **FY 1992 digest:** Department of the Air Force, [United States Air Force Statistical Digest, Fiscal Year 1992](https://archive.org/details/DTIC_ADA551817). Authoritative period context and force totals; it does not provide a complete wing-parent table.
- **Working roster index:** [Air Combat Command predecessor units](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Air_Combat_Command#Predecessor_units_merged_into_Air_Combat_Command_1992). Used only as an index to identify lineage records requiring official cross-checking.
